Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

AI CareerLab (Android package com.airemotelab.ai_cloud_architect) is a career-focused educational application for learning artificial intelligence concepts, completing guided labs and projects, and preparing for interviews.

Privacy summary: AI CareerLab does not require an account, does not contain ads or analytics, and does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. Learning preferences and progress are stored locally on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves limited learning state locally so it can remember your choices and progress. This may include your selected career path, company-domain preference, practice-machine configuration, lesson progress and other app preferences.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information or app-usage information. The production app does not provide account creation, cloud synchronisation, advertising, analytics or remote telemetry.

3. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties because the app does not collect personal data.

4. Permissions and network access

The production app does not request sensitive Android permissions and does not require internet access for its learning content. Google Play may independently process purchase, installation, security and diagnostic information under Google's own terms and privacy policy; that processing is controlled by Google rather than AI CareerLab.

5. Retention and deletion

Local learning data remains on your device until you reset it through available app controls, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Because this data is not sent to us, we cannot remotely access or delete it.

6. Security

Keeping app data local reduces exposure to remote systems. You are responsible for protecting access to your device. No technical method can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

AI CareerLab is designed for adult career learning and is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

9. Contact

For privacy or support questions, email bsindhurdev@gmail.com.
