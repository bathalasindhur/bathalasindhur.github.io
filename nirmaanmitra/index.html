NirmaanMitra

A mobile app for discovering construction experts, material vendors, project ideas, and local enquiry support across Indian cities.

Contact View Privacy Policy

Built for early home-build decisions.

NirmaanMitra brings local discovery, project inspiration, saved workspaces, enquiries, and offline-friendly browsing into one app experience.

Find experts

Browse architects, interior designers, civil engineers, contractors, carpenters, and other construction professionals.

Discover vendors

Compare material suppliers and vendors for finishes, slabs, tiles, cladding, paints, and fixtures.

Manage enquiries

Create project enquiries, track replies, and continue conversations from a shared mobile workspace.

Use local context

Prioritize selected city, nearby known cities, same-region results, and cached data when network access is limited.
